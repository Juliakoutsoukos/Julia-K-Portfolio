About

Designing with curiosity, clarity, and people in mind.

I’m Julia, a recent grad from Michigan State University with a B.A. in information science. My coursework was focused around human-centered design, which sparked a great interest in prototyping, user research, and creating experiences that feel thoughtful, unique, intuitive, and useful.

Background

A student designer building meaningful digital experiences.

My work is rooted in human-centered design and shaped by an interest in how technology fits into everyday life. I enjoy taking ideas from early brainstorming into clearer, more usable outcomes.

Across my projects, I’ve worked on prototyping, interface design, research, and front-end development. I’m especially drawn to work that balances aesthetics with usability and helps people navigate experiences more easily.

I’m still growing as a designer, but that’s part of what excites me. I like exploring new tools, testing ideas, and continuing to refine the way I think about design.
