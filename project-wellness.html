MSU Wellness App

Designing for focus, balance, and clarity

Overview

The MSU Wellness App is a mobile concept designed to help students manage focus, structure their time, and maintain a healthier balance between productivity and rest.

Problem

College students often struggle with maintaining focus and balance throughout the day. Many productivity tools feel overwhelming or difficult to sustain.

Goal

  • Support focused work sessions
  • Create structured routines
  • Reduce cognitive overload

Process

The design process included early sketching, low-fidelity wireframes, and a high-fidelity Figma prototype focused on simplicity and clarity.

Design Decisions

Color was used to indicate different states (focus, break, complete), while the interface remained minimal to reduce distractions.

Outcome

The final design presents a calm, intuitive experience that helps users stay focused without feeling overwhelmed.

Reflection

This project reinforced the importance of simplicity in UX design and how small decisions can shape user experience.
